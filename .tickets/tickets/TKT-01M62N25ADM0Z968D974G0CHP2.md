## Description

`guard` (7.2) and the controller's choice of when to fight: defend itself and its owner against hostile creatures in perception, owners only, on command ("guard me", "stay out of it"). Picks fights it can win from hpFrac and stars, and falls back to flee otherwise.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On command, the bot fights creatures that attack it or its owner
- [ ] It declines fights it would lose and retreats instead
- [ ] stop or "stay out of it" ends combat at once

## Implementation plan

Implement one persistent native escort task owning follow, short pickup/pickable detours, confirmed-target melee and bounded escape. Protect the leader plus configured owners/operators, as the user explicitly selected. Keep child capabilities subject to both plugin safety and welcome.allowedTasks. Observe live AI target identity (the network only replicates have-target, not victim identity), and recent actual hits against Tas; never infer an owner attacker from proximity alone. Reuse conservative unstarred creature eligibility, health/stamina/food/tool limits and PvP-aware bystander safety. Abort detours for leader movement, danger, capacity, timeout or leader loss, then rejoin without competing controller jobs. Stop/replacement/session loss cancels the entire composition with no reconnect resurrection. Expose escort phase/leader/target/reason and exact task-ID arbitration with controller retreat. Add protocol, policy, lifecycle, commands and retreat regressions. Run focused recipes, complete just check ci, commit/review, open a stacked PR, deploy, recover Tas safely and start escorting Nimah if she is visible. Retain honest pending live checks; M2 multi-tree/storage and M3 remain incomplete until demonstrated.
