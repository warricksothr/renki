## Description

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On command, the bot fights creatures that attack it or its owner
- [ ] stop or "stay out of it" ends combat at once
- [ ] Uses positioning, normal stamina budgets and friendly-fire protection while accepting personal risk to defend charges; low HP or multiple attackers alone do not abandon them
- [ ] Goal 3.3: two escort-check summaries with goal33 true; paths linked in a note

Protect the leader plus configured owners/operators, as the user explicitly selected. Keep child capabilities subject to both plugin safety and welcome.allowedTasks. Stop/replacement/session loss cancels the entire composition with no reconnect resurrection. Expose escort phase/leader/target/reason and exact task-ID arbitration with controller retreat. Add protocol, policy, lifecycle, commands and retreat regressions.
