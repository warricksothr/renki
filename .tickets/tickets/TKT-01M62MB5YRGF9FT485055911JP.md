## Description

On 2026-10-28 Nimah shut Tasleroch in the house by closing the door. The bot cannot get out: the walker does not know doors exist, and stuck recovery only jumps, strafes and re-paths.

### What to do

- **While walking:** a closed door within reach, ahead in the direction of travel, is opened the way a player opens one (Door.Interact), so the game's own rules on wards and keys apply.
- **When stuck:** a closed door near the bot is opened before any jump or strafe.
- **Behind it:** a door the bot opened is closed again once it is a few metres past it, so the base stays shut against creatures. Doors that cannot be closed are left alone.
- **Refusals:** a door that will not open (a ward, a key) is traced and left for a while, not retried every frame.
- **The switch:** a BepInEx setting (Safety.AllowDoors, on by default) turns all of this off.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Walking into a closed door opens it, and the bot goes through
- [ ] Stuck beside a closed door, the bot opens it before jumping or strafing
- [ ] A door the bot opened is closed once it is past
- [ ] A door that refuses is traced and not retried every frame
- [ ] Verified in the game: shut in the house, the bot comes out when called
