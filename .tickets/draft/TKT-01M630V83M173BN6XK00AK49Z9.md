## Description

Seen 2026-10-28 (23:09:46): a voice reply landed while a deploy was
logging the bot out. The session had no allowed tasks, so the proposal
and the reply were rejected with not_allowed, and the brain tried to say
"I'm not allowed to do that.", which was also rejected. Nothing reached
the game, but the reason is wrong: the bot was leaving, not forbidden.

Small, but it matters more in M6: once the agent wakes on events, replies arrive without anyone having just spoken, so turns that finish during a logout or a reconnect become common rather than rare.

### Where it happens

- `Session.Logout` in `internal/session/session.go` drops the automation and sends `logout`; `Session.AllowedTasks` then reports nothing permitted, and `Dispatch` returns `ErrNotAllowed`.
- `internal/chat/brain.go` turns `session.ErrNotAllowed` into "I'm not allowed to do that." in several places (the command path around line 674, home around line 888, and `explain` for `ReasonNotAllowed`).

### Approach

Let the session say it is leaving (a logging-out or not-connected state, or a distinct error such as `ErrLeaving` returned by `Dispatch` after `Logout`), and have the brain stay silent, or log only, when a refusal is caused by that state rather than by allowedTasks.

### Files

- `internal/session/session.go`: `Logout`, `Dispatch`, `AllowedTasks`, `ErrNotAllowed`
- `internal/chat/brain.go`: the `ErrNotAllowed` branches and `explain`
- `internal/session/session_test.go`, `internal/chat/brain_test.go`

### Verification

- `just check controller test-packages ./internal/session ./internal/chat`: a voice reply and a command that arrive after `Logout` produce no "not allowed" line and a log record naming the logout; a genuinely disallowed task still gets "I'm not allowed to do that."
- `just check ci`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reply that arrives during a logout is not answered with I'm not allowed to do that
- [ ] A task that is genuinely not allowed still gets I'm not allowed to do that, shown by a test
