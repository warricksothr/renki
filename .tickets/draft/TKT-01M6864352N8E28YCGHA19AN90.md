## Description

### Owner direction

Continue multiple harvests and eligible encounters before returning when measured pressures justify it. Consider cargo value, inventory slots and weight/capacity, food and equipment condition, distance and danger to home or nearest permitted storage, and confidence in the route. Production work is a durable episode above native routines rather than a sequence ending after one tree or enemy.

Maintain the remembered work site and progress; choose work, collection, deposit, resupply or repair from compact host-derived pressure signals. Hysteresis and a committed return phase prevent oscillation. Survival and owner orders interrupt. Verify storage transfers, food restocking and actual tool/armour durability changes before resuming. Missing access, stations, supplies or safe routes produce bounded alternatives and actionable diagnostics.

Repair should use appropriate game stations and be preferred to replacements where possible. TKT-01M64T4WGZA175B38A8GBQ9YD4 (Tools: acquire, craft, repair and upgrade harvesting equipment) provides the equipment prerequisites; include armour maintenance in the native repair capability. Preserve crafting progression and owner resource policy. Pressure computation belongs to the host; scripts select bounded supported routines and cannot relax physical safety.

Design captured in docs/GOALS.md under planned production work and maintenance cycles. Keep the current combat_trial deliberately bounded for verification; this ticket implements production continuation after M2/M3 native routines have trustworthy evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Repeated harvesting, collection and eligible encounters continue until measured return pressures justify a transition
- [ ] Capacity, cargo value, durability, food, danger and route cost/confidence affect the destination choice with hysteresis
- [ ] Matched storage receipts, replenished supplies and verified tool/armour repairs precede resuming the remembered work site
- [ ] Survival/owner interruption, inaccessible stations/storage and uncertain restart receipts cannot create an unbounded loop
