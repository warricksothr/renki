## Description

`guard` (7.2) and the controller's choice of when to fight: defend itself and its owner against hostile creatures in perception, owners only, on command ("guard me", "stay out of it"). Picks fights it can win from hpFrac and stars, and falls back to flee otherwise.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On command, the bot fights creatures that attack it or its owner
- [ ] It declines fights it would lose and retreats instead
- [ ] stop or "stay out of it" ends combat at once

## Implementation plan

Implement self-defense before owner guarding, using a shared fight-or-retreat decision with the existing retreat watcher and body lease. Retreat currently reacts to every confirmed hostile hit even at full health; an independent attack loop would be immediately canceled. Preserve fresh-hit/world scoping and active flee protection while allowing a known low-risk, single-attacker encounter to select bounded melee only with high health (initial conservative 80%), a usable weapon, adequate stamina reserve and supported clear surroundings. Multiple engaged threats, unknown/stronger enemies, failing equipment, unsafe geometry or no progress choose flee. Mere hostile=true is insufficient: actual live Deer observations carry that flag. Require fresh attack/target evidence and never target players/tamed creatures. Health fraction/stars alone cannot estimate victory. Emergency flee outranks combat, which interrupts ordinary work; owner stop/stay-out-of-it clears pending/recent-hit combat intent immediately without disabling existing escape. Never resume stale harvest/haul work after interruption or reconnect. Add mode/threat/reason/stamina/interrupted-job status and stop/retreat controls to the panel. Guard-me comes later with explicit attacker/victim evidence: current damage reporting observes Tas only, and remote AI targets may be unknown. Keep opt-in behavior and plugin AllowCombat separate. Test arbitration/stop suppression before one staged Greyling live trial. See docs/SELF_DEFENSE.md. Ticket remains draft while considering this scope.
